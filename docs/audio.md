# Sound

All sound in Relive the Round is synthesized at runtime with the Web Audio API. There are no audio
files, no samples and no external requests (the CSP stays `default-src 'self'`). The background
music is generated by code from an original chord loop, so it is free of third-party copyright.

## Architecture

Code lives in `web/src/audio/`.

| File | Chunk | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `index.ts` | entry | Facade used by `gamePage.ts`: settings, gesture unlock, scene → music mood, mute, `visibilitychange`. Every call is a no-op until the engine has loaded. |
| `settings.ts` | entry | Pure: `{ muted, music }`, URL overrides, persistence through injected storage. |
| `engine.ts` | lazy | Builds the audio graph and owns the event tracker; loaded with `import("./engine")` on the first user gesture. |
| `events.ts` | lazy | Pure: `track(prev, snapshot)` turns two live-round snapshots into sound events; `replayEvents` for the replay. |
| `pattern.ts` | lazy | Pure: `musicNotes(fromStep, toStep, mood, intense)`. |
| `sfx.ts`, `music.ts`, `synth.ts` | lazy | Sound recipes, music scheduler, oscillator/noise voice helpers. |

Audio graph: `sfxBus` and `musicBus → duck` feed `master → DynamicsCompressor → destination`.
One shared 1 s white-noise buffer is read at random offsets. Sound-effect voices are capped
(`VOICE_CAP` = 12 for low-priority sounds such as footsteps and bot shots, `VOICE_HARD_CAP` = 20).

**Unlock.** Browsers start audio only after a user gesture, and iOS needs the `AudioContext`
created and resumed synchronously inside that gesture. `gamePage.ts` calls `audio.unlock()` from
capture-phase `pointerdown`, `pointerup`, `keydown` and `touchend` listeners (registered before the
kiosk handlers, which may stop the event); it retries on every gesture until the context runs.

**No timers.** Music is scheduled from the page's single frame loop (`audio.frame()`), 300 ms ahead
of the audio clock (`LOOKAHEAD_S`); after a stall it restarts just ahead instead of playing a burst.
No `setInterval`, no AudioWorklet, no blob URLs.

**The sim stays pure.** The audio code only reads snapshots (`SimState`, countdown, clock) that
`gamePage.ts` already has; nothing under `web/src/sim/` imports audio (checked by
`tests/audio-sources.test.ts`).

## Sound list

| Sound | Trigger | Recipe |
|---|---|---|
| Your shot | `stats.shotsFired` grows | band-pass noise 1.8 kHz, 80 ms + square 180 → 60 Hz |
| Bot shot | `state.shots` entry not by `P0` | same, low-passed; gain `1 / (1 + (d / 1500)^2)`, pan `clamp(dx / 2500)`, at least 40 ms apart |
| Hit marker | `stats.hits` grows | sine 1.4 kHz, 40 ms |
| Kill | `stats.kills` grows | sine 880 Hz then 1320 Hz |
| Hurt | `stats.damageTaken` grows while alive | saw 140 Hz + low noise |
| Death | outcome reason `death` | saw sweep 200 → 40 Hz |
| Footstep | every `STEP_CM` (190 cm) walked | low-pass noise 300 Hz, 50 ms; enemies within 1500 cm quieter and panned |
| Defuse | start (0 → 1 tick), every 15 ticks, complete | square blip, rising sine ticks, major arpeggio |
| Enemy defusing | an enemy starts defusing | two low saw warnings |
| Spike beep | spike planted | sine beep at 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 Hz for > 20 / 10–20 / 5–10 / < 5 s left |
| Explosion | outcome reason `detonate` | 1.2 s noise with a low-pass sweep + 60 → 35 Hz sub |
| Win / lose | outcome winner | rising D major / falling minor arpeggio |
| Countdown | `session.countdown` changes | 660 Hz for 3-2-1, 990 Hz for go |
| Button click | any button click inside `#game` (bubble phase) | sine 600 Hz, 30 ms (no hover sounds) |
| Replay scrub | replay slider input | short tick, at most one per 60 ms |

Frames can cover several sim ticks, and `state.shots` only holds the last tick, so the tracker
works on cumulative `stats` deltas (capped at `MAX_REPEAT` = 3 per kind per frame). A new
`Session` object resets the tracker.

**Replay.** Only the YOU panel makes sound (your shots and kills, at `REPLAY_GAIN` = 0.45), at 1×
and 2× speed and only when playback advances normally (not on slider/arrow seeks). The PRO panel is
silent.

## Music

An original loop in D minor over Dm – Bb – Gm – A, 16 steps per bar, 4 bars per cycle.

| Mood | Screens | Tempo | Bus gain | Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `menu` | level select, kiosk attract loop | 92 BPM | 0.28 | pad, sparse arpeggio, soft bass |
| `round` | play | 120 BPM | 0.22 | filtered eighth-note bass, kick, off-beat hat, quiet pad; **intense layer** (sixteenth hats, chord stabs, arpeggio) when the spike is planted or under 10 s are left |
| `calm` | score, replay | 92 BPM | 0.15 | pad and a sparse arpeggio |

Explosions, deaths and the win/lose stingers duck the music by 6 dB for 400 ms (`DUCK_S`).

## Mute and settings

- The speaker button (`#muteToggle`, top-right; the top-left corner is the kiosk operator tap
  zone) or the **M** key toggles mute (`keys.ts`). M is ignored in text entry (text-like inputs
  such as the initials field, textarea, contentEditable), on key repeat and with modifiers; it
  still works on the replay slider. After a pointer click the button drops focus so Space (fire)
  cannot toggle it.
- Muting suspends the `AudioContext`; hiding the tab suspends it as well. Sound counts as audible
  as soon as a resume is requested (resume() is async), so no events are dropped right after
  unmute or tab return; it stops at once on mute/hide and when a resume is refused.
- Normal mode stores `{ muted, music }` in `localStorage` (`rtr.audio.v1`).
- Kiosk mode (`?kiosk=1`) never persists: the choice lasts for the current visitor, and the booth
  returns to sound on when it falls back to the attract loop.
- `?mute` and `?auto` start muted and never persist. `?music=0` turns the music off (effects stay).

## Dev sound counter

In `pnpm dev` only, `window.__rtr.sounds()` returns a copy of a `Record<string, number>` counting
each sound requested since page load (before the voice cap): `shot`, `botShot`, `hit`, `kill`,
`hurt`, `death`, `step` (own), `enemyStep`, `defuseStart`, `defuseTick`, `defuseDone`,
`enemyDefuse`, `beep`, `explosion`, `countdown`, `win`, `lose`, `click`, `scrub`, and `music`
(one bucket for all music notes). Keys appear on first use. It lives in `debugCounts.ts` and is
compiled out of production builds (`import.meta.env.DEV`).

## Bundle size

The facade, settings and mute-key helper add about 1.5 KB gzip to the entry chunk (gzip -9:
125,979 B before, 127,505 B after). The engine chunk (`engine-*.js`, effects + music + tracker) is about 3.7 KB gzip and
is only downloaded after the first gesture, and not at all while muted.
